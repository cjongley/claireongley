Hi, I'm Claire.

I design AI agents for use by staff at Inner West Council, and teach people how to use AI safely and ethically. After work, I'm almost always making something - whether it's with yarn, fabric or on paper.

what i do

Training, agent builds, and the docs holding it together.

At Inner West Council, I design and build Microsoft Copilot Studio agents, document how they work, and train the people who'll actually use them. Most days that means turning a clunky manual process into something a colleague can just ask a question and get a straight answer from.

  • Training Facilitation

    Running sessions that teach staff how to actually use Copilot safely and ethically.

    • Facilitated training for over 500 staff in AI Ethics and Copilot Chat
    • Facilitated training for 100 staff in advanced Copilot usage
  • Agent builds

    Designing and building Copilot Studio agents that solve real workflow problems, from performance objectives to test strategy packs.

    Here are some of the 12 agents I've built:

    • EOI Scope Assistant
    • Facilities Agent
    • Test Strategy Assistant
  • Technical hub

    Maintaining the internal knowledge base that documents what we've learned, so it doesn't live in one person's head.
